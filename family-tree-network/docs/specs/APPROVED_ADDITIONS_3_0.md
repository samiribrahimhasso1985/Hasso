## Owner's statement on the product's distinctiveness
A person on FAMILY NETWORK is not just a social account: they own a connected personal, family, professional and
public identity, and can turn that identity into real relationships, collaboration opportunities and knowledge.

## Approved new additions (names as given; definitions proposed — to be confirmed)
### 1. Personal & family life
| Addition | Proposed interpretation |
|---|---|
| **My Life Map — خريطة حياتي** | The places of one's life over time (born, studied, lived, worked) on a map, built from Life Timeline entries; country/city precision only, per-entry audience. |
| **My Legacy Story — قصتي للأجيال** | A personal story/memoir for future generations — the personal part of Family Legacy (v2 §16), with explicit stewardship and release rules. |
| **Family Organizer — منظم العائلة** | Shared family planning inside a family community: tasks, shared lists, responsibilities, reminders; integrates with Events. |
| **Family Documents — وثائق العائلة** | Shared family documents — a section of the Family Vault (sitemap §15), with encryption, access permissions and access history; no identity documents without lawful purpose. |
| **Family Reunion — لقاء العائلة** | Already specified (v2 §13): hybrid reunions, invitations across countries, program, optional live stream. |
| **Family Knowledge — معرفة العائلة** | Collective family knowledge (traditions, recipes, skills, advice, language/dialect) contributed by members, distinguished from verified history. |

### 2. Business & partnerships
| Addition | Proposed interpretation |
|---|---|
| **Business Partner Finder — البحث عن شريك عمل** | Opt-in matching of people by skills, sector, needs and offers from Professional Identity (§32); contact only through requests. |
| **Startup Hub — مركز المشاريع الناشئة** | Startup pages (organization type), team building, mentors, investors (opt-in), events; no investment offers without legal review. |

### 3. Media & production
| Addition | Proposed interpretation |
|---|---|
| **FN Interview Studio — استوديو المقابلات** | Production tool for recorded/live interviews (multi-guest room, recording with consent, publishing to Public Videos) — the production side of interviews and FN Originals; Celebrity Studio remains the celebrity's management dashboard. |
| **FN Webinars — الندوات الإلكترونية** | Webinars as a session type of the shared live engine (registration, Q&A, optional tickets via module 34). |
