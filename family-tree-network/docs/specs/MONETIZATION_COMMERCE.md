# Monetization, Creator Economy & Commerce System — Module 34 (Global Revenue Ecosystem)

> Source: product owner. Stored as CLAUDE.md §34. Consolidated into `FAMILY_NETWORK_MASTER_SPECIFICATION_3.0.md`.

## 1. Objective
A complete revenue ecosystem allowing: (1) FAMILY NETWORK to generate platform revenue · (2) creators to earn · (3) celebrities to monetize audiences · (4) businesses to sell products and services · (5) users to open personal stores · (6) organizations to promote products · (7) customers to buy physical and digital products · (8) event organizers to sell tickets. All monetization runs through **one unified account and payment architecture**.

## 2. Core revenue modules
FN Live Monetization · FN Creator Earnings · FN Celebrity Earnings · FN Market · FN Official Store · Creator Stores · Business Stores · FN Coins · FN Ads & Partnerships · FN Bookings · FN Premium · FN Originals · FN Financial Dashboard.

## 3. Live monetization
- **A. Live Gifts:** paid digital gifts · transparent prices · creator earnings · platform commission · gift history · refund and fraud policies.
- **B. Paid Live:** ticketed broadcasts · private events · educational sessions · exclusive interviews · paid audience access.
- **C. Live Membership:** monthly membership · exclusive sessions · member badges · member-only content.
- **D. Live Shopping:** product display · descriptions · links · cart · secure checkout · order confirmation.
- **E. Sponsored Live:** brand sponsorship · sponsored segments · clearly disclosed advertising · campaign analytics.

## 4. Creator & celebrity earnings
Dashboard: total revenue · available balance · pending balance · gifts · memberships · paid live · advertising · sponsorship · video revenue · product sales · bookings · transaction fees · refunds · payout history. Configurable payout thresholds and approved payout methods. **Never guarantee earnings or display fabricated revenue.**

## 5. FN Market
Store types: A FN Official Store · B Creator Stores · C Celebrity Stores · D Business Stores · E Professional Service Stores · F Digital Products.
Categories: clothing · accessories · books · electronics accessories · home products · art · collectibles · educational materials · digital courses · digital templates · creator merchandise · official FAMILY NETWORK merchandise.

## 6. Creator stores
Eligible creators request a store. Management: name · logo · banner · description · catalog · product photos · prices · inventory · variants · discounts · shipping options · return policy · order management · customer messages · sales analytics. Store linked to the public profile; **Store tab** on eligible creator and celebrity profiles; products linkable to public videos and live broadcasts.

## 7. Product management
Fields: product ID · seller ID · title · description · category · SKU · price · currency · stock · images · variants · shipping regions · delivery estimates · return conditions · status · created date.
Types: physical · digital · made-to-order · limited editions · pre-orders where legally permitted. Products prohibited by law or marketplace policy are not allowed.

## 8. Marketplace transactions
Cart · checkout · payment authorization · confirmation · order management · seller and buyer notifications · refunds · disputes · shipping status · delivery confirmation · seller payouts. Use an appropriate payment service provider and marketplace payment solution. **Never store raw card details.** Provider webhooks and idempotent transaction processing.

## 9. Commissions (configurable)
Proposed test defaults (not final terms): live gifts 70/30 · paid live tickets 85/15 · memberships 85/15 · marketplace sales 90/10 · digital courses 85/15 · direct sponsorship management 80/20 (creator or seller / platform).
Final amounts account for payment processing costs, taxes, refunds, chargebacks, shipping, production costs, contractual agreements. Rules configurable by product type, region and agreement. **Full fee breakdown shown before a seller accepts a transaction.**

## 10. FN Coins
Internal virtual currency bought through supported payment providers; used for eligible digital gifts and selected features. Required: purchase history · balance · gift transactions · spending limits · refund policy · fraud detection · account protection. Coins are distinct from withdrawable cash balances; never presented as investments; no unrestricted peer-to-peer cash transfers; extra safeguards and parental permissions for minors.

## 11. FN Ads & Partnerships
Advertisers: create campaigns · budgets · periods · eligible audience categories · submit material · select creators · review performance. Creators: receive offers · negotiate · accept/reject · publish sponsored content · review payments. Sponsored content clearly labelled; tools for advertising permissions, disclosure and campaign records.

## 12. FN Bookings
Paid appointments and services: consulting · educational sessions · interviews · workshops · professional services · private video meetings · group sessions. Features: service catalog · price · duration · calendar · booking · payment · cancellation rules · refund rules · notifications · meeting integration · reviews. Extra qualification checks for regulated professional services.

## 13. FN Originals
Official media production brand: interviews · documentaries · talk shows · educational programs · business programs · live events · creator collaborations. Revenue: sponsorship · advertising · paid access · licensing · memberships · merchandise. Production budgets, rights management, contributor agreements, revenue reports.

## 14. Financial dashboards
**Creator:** total earnings · by source · monthly performance · product sales · live performance · sponsorships · pending payouts · available balance.
**Platform:** gross transaction volume · platform revenue · marketplace commission · subscription revenue · advertising revenue · live revenue · payment fees · refunds · chargebacks · operating costs · net revenue. Keep GMV, platform revenue and company profit separate.

## 15. Payments and compliance
Designed for European and international requirements: GDPR · consumer protection · Digital Services Act where applicable · VAT and other taxes · seller identity and business verification · marketplace seller obligations · advertising disclosures · cancellation and return rights · digital content consent and withdrawal rules · payment provider requirements · accounting and invoices · age restrictions · copyright and authenticity. **Professional legal and tax review before enabling transactions in each market.**

## 16. Security and fraud
Seller verification · buyer protection · suspicious transaction monitoring · refund management · chargebacks · payout risk controls · audit trails · account takeover protection · dispute workflows. No high-risk payouts before required checks. Creators/sellers cannot manipulate reviews or sales metrics.

## 17. Implementation phases (module-internal)
1 Earnings ledger · creator dashboard · payment provider integration · paid memberships · basic paid live events.
2 FN Market · creator stores · product catalog · checkout · order management.
3 Live gifts · FN Coins · live shopping · creator and celebrity monetization.
4 Advertising · sponsorship marketplace · FN Bookings · business stores.
5 FN Originals · advanced analytics · global seller support · international payouts.

## 18. Final acceptance criteria
Every eligible creator can view earnings · every seller can manage products · customers purchase securely · every transaction has a traceable ledger entry · refunds and disputes supported · commission calculations accurate · private payment information protected · reports distinguish gross and net · users can access transaction history · all financial features tested before public launch.

**Final principle:** FAMILY NETWORK should create value for the platform, creators, celebrities, businesses and customers. The platform earns when it provides real value; creators earn when they create value; sellers earn when they sell real products or services.

**Reminder (owner):** videos stay in exactly two sections — Public Videos and Family Videos. No My Video Archive, no Friends Videos.
