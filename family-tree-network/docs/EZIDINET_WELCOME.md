# EZIDINET Welcome (v1.2)

Status: **proposal** · Phase C (first community module recommended — low cost, high value).

## Purpose
Welcome and orientation services for community members in different countries — newcomers, new arrivals in a country,
young people, returning members.

## Content
* **Platform welcome:** first-steps tour, privacy explained in plain language (Kurmanji, Arabic, German, English).
* **Country guides** (per country, community-written, human-reviewed): language courses, community institutions
  (from WORLD directory), cultural calendar, practical orientation links to **official sources** (authorities,
  counselling services). Not legal advice; every guide shows "last reviewed" date and reviewer.
* **Welcome circles:** opt-in groups of volunteers ("guides") who help newcomers in a city/country. Contact only after
  the newcomer requests it; volunteers are verified members with an accepted code of conduct.
* **Mentoring link** to professional/freelance skills (`EZIDINET_FREELANCE.md`).

## Safety
* Newcomers can be vulnerable (asylum procedures, language barriers): no public "newcomer" label; nobody can search for
  newcomers; volunteers see only what the newcomer shares.
* No collection of residence status or asylum data.
* Reports and blocking available in every conversation.

## Screens
Welcome home · Country guide · Find a guide (request) · Volunteer as guide (application + code of conduct) · My welcome
requests.

## Data (planning)
`country_guides`, `guide_sections`, `guide_reviews`, `welcome_volunteers`, `welcome_requests`.
