# EZID Kids — Educational Content Framework (v1.1)

## 1. Content areas
| Area | Examples of topics (to be defined by the cultural review group) |
|---|---|
| Identity & culture | who we are, family and community, respect for elders, hospitality |
| Traditions & customs | everyday customs, clothing, food, music, crafts |
| Festivals & celebrations | the main Yazidi festivals and their meaning, age-adapted |
| Symbols & meanings | cultural symbols explained simply and respectfully |
| Values | respect, cooperation, coexistence, honesty, kindness, courage |
| Language | Kurmanji alphabet (Latin), words, phrases, songs, rhymes |
| Geography & places | landscapes, villages, landmarks, nature, diaspora around the world |
| History | age-appropriate history; sensitive periods only for 13–16 with guidance |

The list above is a structure, not content: **all actual cultural and religious content is written or approved by
qualified community contributors**, never invented by developers or AI.

## 2. Classification of every content item
| Label | Meaning | Shown to children as |
|---|---|---|
| DOCUMENTED | supported by cited sources | "What we know" |
| ORAL_TRADITION | passed down orally in families/communities | "A story our elders tell" |
| INTERPRETATION | one of several views in the community | "Some people believe…" (other views acknowledged) |
| GENERAL | language, colours, nature, values | — |

Disputed interpretations are never presented as universally accepted; regional and family diversity is respected.

## 3. Sensitive topics
* The persecution of the community (including the 2014 genocide) is **not shown to ages 3–8**; for 9–12 only gentle,
  hope-focused framing with guardian notes; for 13–16 factual, non-graphic content with guidance and support links
  for guardians.
* No graphic images, no descriptions of violence, no content that could re-traumatise.
* Guardians can block any topic.

## 4. Content lifecycle
Draft (contributor) → cultural review (accuracy, classification, sources) → safety review (age fit, trauma, privacy)
→ publish (admin, per age group and language) → periodic re-review → retire. Every step recorded
(`kids_content_reviews`).

## 5. Per-item metadata
title (4 languages) · section · age groups · learning goals · classification · sources · sensitivity · media (images,
audio with narrator consent, illustrations with licence) · reviewers · version.

## 6. Media rules
Original illustrations or properly licensed works only · no photos of real children · audio narrated by consenting
adults (native speakers) · captions/transcripts for accessibility · credit contributors where they wish.

## 7. First content inventory (proposal for the community to prepare before Phase 7)
20 Kurmanji alphabet cards with audio · 100 basic words in themes · 10 short stories (values) · 6 festival explainers ·
3 songs · 30 quiz questions per age group 6–8 and 9–12 · 10 coloring pages with cultural patterns.
