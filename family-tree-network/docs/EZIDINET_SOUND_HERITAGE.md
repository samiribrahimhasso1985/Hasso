# EZIDINET Sound Heritage (v1.2)

Status: **proposal** · part of the Heritage module (`EZIDINET_HERITAGE.md`) · Phase H.

## Purpose
Audio archive for music, songs, oral histories and recordings — preserving voices with consent.

## Collections
Music & songs · Oral histories (interviews, life stories, testimonies) · Spoken word (stories, proverbs, prayers where
the community allows publication) · Language recordings (pronunciation, dialects — also used by EZIDINET Kids Languages
with permission).

## Screens
Sound home · Player (speed, transcript side-by-side, chapters) · Recording page (performer/teller, recorder, date,
region, language/dialect, knowledge status, licence) · Record or upload (with consent step) · Transcription editor
(human-reviewed) · Reviewer queue.

## Consent & rights (mandatory)
* Every identifiable voice: consent record of the speaker (or guardian / heirs for archive material), scope (archive
  only · members · public · educational · AI indexing) and the right to withdraw.
* **Testimonies of persecution and violence** (e.g. survivors' accounts) are highly sensitive: default visibility
  *restricted*; publication only with explicit informed consent; option to anonymise (voice alteration, name removal);
  trauma-informed review; never used to train or prompt AI without separate consent.
* Music: performer and composer rights recorded; commercial recordings only with licence.
* **No voice cloning / synthetic voices of real people** (applies to AI Voice Archive, `EZIDINET_AI_ECOSYSTEM.md`).

## AI support (optional, reviewed)
Automatic transcription and translation as drafts; a human reviewer approves before publication.

## Storage
Original files in private object storage; streaming derivatives (Opus/AAC) via signed short-lived URLs.

## Phase-1 scope
Upload + consent + review + player + manual transcript. Recording in-app and AI transcription later.
